FAQ

Can we park a kit van? Overnight too?
Yes. The car park on the property is wide and free, and takes both vans and pickups loaded with booth materials — overnight for the whole stay. Tell us how many vehicles on LINE when you book so we can keep enough space for you.
The team gets back very late after teardown. Can we still get in?
Yes. Staff are on the desk 24 hours, so however late you check in or come back, someone is there. There is no lock-out time. If you know in advance it will be a late night, just tell us on LINE.
Do you discount several nights or several rooms?
There are special rates for teams staying longer and booking multiple rooms. The price depends on the number of nights, the number of rooms, and whether the dates fall on a major event. Message @Baanpermsook on LINE with the show name, dates and room count and we will quote you within minutes.
What time is breakfast? Will we make it to the hall?
Breakfast is included in the room rate and is served early enough to eat and still open the booth on time. If your team needs to leave unusually early, tell us in advance and we will arrange it.
How far is IMPACT? How long is the drive?
IMPACT Muang Thong Thani (Arena / Challenger / Thunder Dome) is roughly 7-10 minutes away, about 3.8 km. Close enough to come back for a midday break or to fetch something you forgot.
How do we book? Is a deposit required?
LINE @Baanpermsook is fastest, or fill in the form below and we will get back to you. You can also call 094-962-5955 at any hour. For multi-room bookings, deposit terms are easiest to agree over LINE.
